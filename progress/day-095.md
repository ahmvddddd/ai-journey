## Lessons Learned

* **Dynamic Dimension Wiring:** Calculating flattened feature widths programmatically from tracked spatial dimensions (`prev_c * spatial * spatial`) prevents brittle hardcoded linear layer sizes when experimenting with image resolutions or pooling depths.

* **Logits-First Classification Heads:** Returning unnormalized logits from the final `nn.Linear` layer preserves numerical stability inside `nn.CrossEntropyLoss` and keeps inference flexible for `torch.softmax` probability calibration or `torch.argmax` class selection.
