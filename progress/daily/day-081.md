## Lessons Learned

* **Module Hook Execution:** Invoking `model(x)` triggers `nn.Module.__call__`, ensuring forward pre-hooks, forward hooks, and backward autograd hooks execute properly rather than being bypassed.
* **Unbounded Regression Outputs:** Omitting non-linear clamping activations like `ReLU` on the final regression linear layer prevents zero-gradient dead zones and allows predictions across the full continuous range.
