## Lessons Learned

* **Rank-Order Robustness:** Percentiles and IQR rely on positional sorting rather than numerical magnitude, making them immune to extreme outlier distortion unlike the mean and standard deviation.
* **Tail Latency Amplification:** In distributed architectures with high fan-out, compounding probabilities mean that individual microservice reliability can result in a high percentage of users experiencing sluggish requests.
* **Interpolation Nuances:** Different programming libraries use varying quantile definitions (Hyndman-Fan algorithms) for small sample sizes, leading to minor floating-point interpolation discrepancies.
