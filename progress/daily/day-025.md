## Lessons Learned

* **Density vs. Probability:** PDF values can exceed 1 because they represent probability density (probability per unit) rather than direct point probability, provided the total area under the curve integrates to 1.
* **Skewness Relationships:** In right-skewed distributions, extreme values in the tail pull the arithmetic mean above the median, whereas left-skewed distributions pull the mean below the median.
* **Transformation Necessity:** Feeding raw right-skewed features into linear models degrades predictive accuracy; log transformations or tree-based alternatives are required to normalize feature impact.
