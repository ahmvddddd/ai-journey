## Lessons Learned

* **Multiplicative Asymmetry:** Even when individual features (like price) are uniformly distributed, multiplying them by quantities can introduce right skewness in composite metrics like total revenue.
* **Robust Spread Metrics:** $\text{IQR}$ provides a stable measure of dispersion that complements standard deviation when analyzing financial distributions.
