## Lessons Learned

* **Unbounded Regression Outputs:** Keeping the final `nn.Linear(prev_dim, 1)` layer without `ReLU` avoids zero-gradient dead outputs and leaves the full continuous prediction range open.
* **Train vs. Eval Mode Hygiene:** `nn.Dropout` applies random zeroing and inverted scaling ($1 / (1 - p)$) during `model.train()` but acts as a deterministic identity pass-through during `model.eval()`.
