## Lessons Learned

* **Fail-Fast Dataset Contracts:** Validating row counts and checking `np.isnan()` inside `Dataset.__init__` prevents silent `NaN` gradient propagation and mid-epoch worker crashes before training begins.
* **Rank & Broadcasting Hygiene:** Aligning regression targets to `(batch_size, 1)` guarantees strict 1-to-1 elementwise loss calculation against `(batch_size, 1)` model outputs.
