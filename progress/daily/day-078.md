## Lessons Learned

* **Autograd & Device Hygiene:** Calling `.numpy()` directly on tensors with `requires_grad=True` or on non-CPU devices fails, making `.detach().cpu().numpy()` a reliable conversion pattern.
* **Inner Dimension Alignment:** Matrix multiplication between `(64, 8)` and `(8, 16)` requires matching inner dimensions (`8`) and outputs shape `(64, 16)`.
