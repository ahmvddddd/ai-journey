## Lessons Learned

* **Explicit Axis Permutation Hygiene:** Image loaders (PIL, OpenCV, Matplotlib) and NumPy arrays store channels last (`HWC`), whereas `nn.Conv2d` expects channels first (`NCHW`); always permute axes explicitly rather than reshaping directly, which scrambles spatial pixels across channels.

* **Two-Stage Pixel Scaling:** Dividing `uint8` values by `255.0` prevents integer modulo wrap-around and aligns pixel intensities to `[0.0, 1.0]` before applying dataset-level channel mean and standard deviation normalization.
