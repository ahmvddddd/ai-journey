## Lessons Learned

* **Isolation Discipline:** Recognizing that fitting scalers or encoders on the entire dataset before splitting causes data leakage, resulting in optimistically inflated test scores and production failure.
* **Scale Discrepancies:** Understanding how massive monetary magnitudes (`price_ngn`) can dominate optimization losses if left unstandardized relative to proportional rates (`vat_rate`).
