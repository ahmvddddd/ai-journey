## Lessons Learned

* **Visual Calibration Beyond Scalar Metrics:** Combining Predicted vs. Actual scatter plots with zero-centered residual histograms exposes systematic upper-tail underprediction and bias patterns that aggregate scalar metrics (`RMSE`, `MAE`) can obscure.
