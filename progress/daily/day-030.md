## Lessons Learned

* **Contextual Imputation:** Group-wise imputation (`transform("median")`) preserves category pricing distributions much better than applying a single global median or mean.
* **Modular Pipeline Design:** Wrapping data cleaning logic in a clean function ensures reproducibility across multiple datasets or incoming batch streams.
