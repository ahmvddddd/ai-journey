## Lessons Learned

* **Mean vs. Median for Skewed Data:** In right-skewed distributions (such as housing markets or server latencies), the mean exceeds the median due to extreme high values. The median is a more representative measure of a "typical" data point[cite: 17].
* **Linear Shift Invariance:** Adding a constant $c$ to every data point shifts the mean, median, and mode by $+c$ without altering the shape or spread of the distribution[cite: 17].
* **Distributed Complexity:** Computing a global mean is easily map-reduced via local sums and counts. Calculating an exact global median requires global ranking, making approximate quantile algorithms preferred for massive datasets[cite: 17].
