## Lessons Learned

* **Sample-Weighted Batch Metrics:** Multiplying batch mean losses by `batch_size` before dividing by `total_samples` ensures partial trailing batches do not receive disproportionate weight in epoch summaries.

* **Complementary Loss & Evaluation Metrics:** Quadratic error (`nn.MSELoss`) penalizes large demand spikes strongly during gradient updates, while `MAE` reports average error directly in `demand_units`.
