## Lessons Learned

* **Explicit Precision & Shape Control:** Casting NumPy arrays to `torch.float32` and reshaping 1D regression targets via `.reshape(-1, 1)` eliminates runtime dtype errors and silent loss-broadcasting bugs.
* **Single Responsibility Design:** Keeping per-sample retrieval deterministic and lightweight inside `__getitem__` ensures seamless scaling when integrating background `DataLoader` workers.
