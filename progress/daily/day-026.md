## Lessons Learned

* **Scale Limitation of Covariance:** Covariance indicates the direction of association but lacks bounds, making cross-dataset comparisons impossible until standardized into correlation coefficients.
* **Pearson vs. Spearman:** Pearson evaluates linear fit and is sensitive to outliers and non-linear curves, whereas Spearman evaluates relative rank ordering and handles monotonic non-linear relationships effectively.
* **Multicollinearity Hazard:** Perfect correlation between independent features renders $X^T X$ non-invertible, requiring explicit feature selection or L2 regularization to stabilize model training.
